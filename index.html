What we do

Cut The Bit edits short vertical clips from long-form videos and publishes them on our own YouTube channels, with each clip credited to its original creator as the creator's campaign requires.

Cut The Bit Uploader

Cut The Bit Uploader is our internal tool. It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload our finished clips to the YouTube channels we own;
  • read view counts and YouTube Analytics for those channels, so we can see which clips work.

It is used only by the Cut The Bit team on our own channels. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
