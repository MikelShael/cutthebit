Privacy policy

For the Cut The Bit Uploader. Last updated 11 November 2026.

Who uses the app

Cut The Bit Uploader is an internal tool used only by the Cut The Bit team to manage our own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When a team member signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions for the channel they choose:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish our clips to that channel;
  • Read the channel (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's videos and view counts;
  • Read YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read reports such as views by country for that channel.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish our clips and to measure how they perform.
  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the team's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server we run.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not access, collect or store data from YouTube viewers or any other users.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to the data Google processes.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time on the Google security settings page. Withdrawing it stops all further access. Stored statistics for that channel are then deleted from the team's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Cut The Bit through the "About" section of our YouTube channel.
